06 · HOW IT RUNS

The employer opens the door. The employee produces the signal.

01

The company enrols

HR adds HomeDen to the benefits stack and asks employees to install it. Eligibility comes across from
HRIS.

02

The employee scans

The employee photographs their own mouth, and their covered family's, then repeats on a cadence.

03

The employee gets something back

Coverage detail and remaining balance, in-network clinics, AI-assisted booking, and their score history.

04

ICLO joins the claims side

Eligibility and dental claims are pulled from the TPA and matched to activity — member-month, CDT,
intervention, outcome.

05

The company sees the population

Descriptive statistics on employee oral health, expected dental spend, and spend already incurred.

Consent is per member and revocable. Raw images never leave ICLO's controlled storage; the employer receives aggregates only.
