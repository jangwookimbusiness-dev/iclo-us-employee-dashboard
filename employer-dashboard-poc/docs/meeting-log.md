**What is known.** Snowflake responded positively and a collaboration proposal is under discussion
(J. Kim, reported 2026-08-15, conversation was the previous day).

**What this moves.** H3 is the only live hypothesis in the PRD and its bar is *at least one written
follow-up toward US HLS GTM routing*. A discussion in progress is movement toward that bar, not the
bar itself. It also bears on an open question in the CEO plan — design-partner acquisition was listed
as *broker / direct / Snowflake introduction, undecided*. This makes the third path more plausible.

**What this does not move.** Employer demand. Interviews with US benefits consultants or employers
remain at zero and §5.2 stays blocking. Snowflake is a channel and an infrastructure partner; they do
not buy dental benefits for a workforce.

**Gaps, in the order they are worth closing.**

1. **Is anything written?** H3 is measured in writing, not in warmth. An email, a proposal document, a
   meeting invitation — any of those cross the bar; a good conversation does not.
2. **Who, by name and role.** "Snowflake said" is not a person. Whether this came from an account team,
   an HLS specialist, or an architect changes what it is worth and who to go back to.
3. **What did they raise first?** Before we described the product. That is the only line in this log
   that counts as demand evidence, and it is the one that fades fastest.
